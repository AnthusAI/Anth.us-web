<section class="section section--band" id="our-values"><div class="container section__grid"><header class="section__head"><p class="eyebrow"><span>04</span> <span>/</span> <span>Values</span></p>
<h2>How we decide</h2>
</header>
<div class="section__body"><ul class="principles principles--auto"><li><span>01</span><strong>Prioritize solutions over tools</strong><p>Products and services only deliver business value if you’re in the business of products and services. We’re in the business of solutions.</p>
</li>
<li><span>02</span><strong>Focus on business logic</strong><p>The only code you should write is the business logic that solves real problems. <a href="/blog/langchain-by-example/">Don’t waste time reinventing wheels.</a></p>
</li>
<li><span>03</span><strong>Continuously improve</strong><p>Rapid, iterative change through CI/CD and DevOps, then let the systems improve themselves. Self-optimizing classifiers and self-steering agent systems get better from production feedback without waiting for an engineer to notice.</p>
</li>
<li><span>04</span><strong>Infrastructure as code</strong><p>DevOps for infrastructure, MLOps and LLMOps for the models. Every part of a production system, including training runs and evaluations, should be created by code so it’s reproducible, not clicked together by hand.</p>
</li>
<li><span>05</span><strong>Optimize resource usage</strong><p>Balance efficiency with cost-effectiveness. When intelligence is cheap, the goal shifts from conserving compute to conserving context and cognitive load.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<section class="section" id="loop"><div class="container section__grid"><header class="section__head"><p class="eyebrow"><span>05</span> <span>/</span> <span>February 2026</span></p>
<h2>The night we stopped being the loop</h2>
</header>
<div class="section__body"><div class="prose"><p>When we started using AI to write the code and make the decisions, the chat way of working broke under the volume. A person had to sit there keeping the agents going or the whole process stopped, and a person had to hold in their head what was happening across every parallel session in every project for every client, because nothing else did. The low point is on the record, like everything else we do.</p>
</div>
<blockquote class="post"><p>Stupid new nightly routine that I never wanted: Pasting “Continue.” into Codex 20 times in a row before I go to bed, in four different sessions. I will look back at this like black and white television. Rotary phone dials.</p>
<footer>Ryan Porter (@RyanAlynPorter), <a href="https://x.com/RyanAlynPorter/status/2019302489011696010">February 5, 2026</a></footer>
</blockquote><div class="prose"><p>So we stopped being the loop. We ran all of it on our own work first, in public.</p>
</div>
<ul class="grid grid--thirds"><li class="card notch  card--link"><h3><a href="/platform/tactus/">Tactus</a></h3>
<p>Names the three ways a person can be in the loop, supervised, unattended and asynchronous, and makes the third one programmable: an agent runs on its own and interrupts a human only at a decision point.</p>
</li>
<li class="card notch  card--link"><h3><a href="/platform/kanbus/">Kanbus</a></h3>
<p>The memory that lets work move between Claude Code, Codex, or whatever comes next. We’ve held since 2023 that models are commodities.</p>
</li>
<li class="card notch  card--link"><h3><a href="/platform/primus/">Primus</a></h3>
<p>The scorecard where reviewers correct a decision model’s verdicts and their explanations become policy.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<ul class="grid grid--qa"><li class="card notch card--qa card--static"><h3>What does Anthus build?</h3>
<p>Production AI systems that improve from feedback: fine-tuned classifiers, agent systems, and the evaluation and MLOps infrastructure around them. We also operate what we build.</p>
</li>
<li class="card notch card--qa card--static"><h3>How is Anthus different from a typical AI consultancy?</h3>
<p>We are operators first. The team has run a revenue-critical platform since 2007, through data center failures and DDoS attacks, before applying the same discipline to AI. We ship systems with human-in-the-loop feedback, not demos.</p>
</li>
<li class="card notch card--qa card--static"><h3>What is human-in-the-loop AI?</h3>
<p>Experts review and correct the AI’s judgments, and those corrections become training signal. In the Call Criteria classifier lab, QA specialists guide the models this way, and the scorecards have improved through reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) over years of production, across hundreds of scorecards and millions of interactions.</p>
</li>
<li class="card notch card--qa card--static"><h3>Do you only work with one model provider?</h3>
<p>No. We treat models as replaceable components and design systems so a model can be swapped without rebuilding the business logic.</p>
